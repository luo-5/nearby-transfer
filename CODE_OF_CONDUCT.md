# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

Participants should be able to contribute without harassment, intimidation, or
discrimination. We welcome constructive disagreement about technical decisions while
requiring respect for the people involved.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, patient, and specific in reviews and bug reports.
- Critique code and decisions rather than people.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, licenses, and attribution.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to propose alternatives.
- Help make the project usable for people with different backgrounds and experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publishing another person's private or
security-sensitive information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict
interaction, or permanently ban participation depending on impact and recurrence.
Good-faith security research reported through the documented private process is not a
conduct violation.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through the contact options on
[`@luo-5`'s GitHub profile](https://github.com/luo-5). If the concern includes a
security disclosure, use the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports
will be handled as confidentially as the available channel permits. A maintainer who
is the subject of a report must not make the sole enforcement decision.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, project
communication channels, and public representation of Nearby Transfer.
